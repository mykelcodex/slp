About SLP Events

We don't do events.
We do moments.

Stay Lit Party started as a promise between friends: nobody leaves our parties early. It became a company that keeps that promise for hundreds of clients a year.

The Story

Born at a party that refused to end

It began with a single pair of cold spark machines and a borrowed van. The first wedding we lit, the couple's first dance stopped the room — phones up, jaws down. The next morning, three of their guests called us.

Word of mouth built SLP Events into a full production house: interactive performers, luxury effects, marquee letters, and a team that treats a backyard birthday with the same reverence as a ballroom gala. The van is nicer now. The promise hasn't changed.

Safety First, Always

Spectacular doesn't mean risky

Every effect we run is chosen specifically because it's safe around guests. Cold spark machines produce sparks you could pass a hand through. Our clouds are food-grade dry ice. Our confetti is flame-retardant and biodegradable.

We carry comprehensive liability insurance, provide certificates to your venue before the event, and staff a trained technician on every machine, all night.

Setup, Invisible

You'll never see us coming

BEFORE

We coordinate with your venue, walk the floor plan, and stage equipment 2–3 hours ahead — hidden from guests.

DURING

A technician runs each effect on a cue sheet synced with your DJ and photographer. Nothing fires early. Nothing fires late.

AFTER

We tear down quietly and leave the venue exactly as we found it — confetti cleanup included, always.
